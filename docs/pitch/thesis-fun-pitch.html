Demo · AI × options

An agent that explains, proposes, and never signs.

  • Reads the live book and the thesis
  • Prepares real calldata, same path as the ticket
  • Hard loss ceiling and daily caps on the server
  • Your wallet approves every transaction
This is the AI × Options track. Be precise: the agent prepares, the human signs. Autonomous signing is on the roadmap as a session-key design, deliberately not shipped for a hackathon with real money.
Why this has to be onchain

Rip out the chain and the product is a Discord with screenshots.

Without the chain

  • Track records are screenshots
  • Losing calls quietly disappear
  • "Copy me" needs a custodian

On Base with Thetanuts

  • Every position is a real option, verifiable by tx
  • A losing thesis cannot be deleted
  • Your wallet is your identity and your custody
The blockchain-necessity test, answered with one sentence: the feed is only worth something because nobody, including us, can fake a fill.

What we could not finish · the roadmap

What is next, in order.

Shipped: the MVP on mainnet

Every liquid market, both sides, verified feed, share cards, the approval-gated agent, the first real fill.

Next: creator payouts and recovery

Pay the author of a thesis from the fees their post drives. A one-tap recovery for a fill whose record was lost mid-flight.

Then: every collateral, autonomous hedging

cbBTC and WETH collateral once contract sizes are proven with real fills. Session-key execution so the agent can hedge as your account, never as its own wallet.

A bottom-sheet ticket, push on settlement, and any options venue with a public order book.
